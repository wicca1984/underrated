Spider-Man Saves the Day Again

By Peter Parker

Yesterday afternoon, a spectacular rescue took place in midtown Manhattan when a runaway construction crane was secured safely.

Witnesses reported seeing the masked hero swing from a nearby skyscraper to secure the collapsing crane before it struck the crowded street below.

Authorities have expressed their gratitude, though some city officials remain skeptical of the vigilante's methods.
