handles `CssValue::Length(v, unit)`. Compute the raised-amount `raise` (positive = raised) then push
`total_shift += -raise` (negative shift = up), matching the sign convention above:
- `LengthUnit::Px` or `LengthUnit::Pt`: `raise = v` (treat pt as px, consistent with the rest of the
  engine which already collapses non-px absolute units to px in `get_font_size`).
- `LengthUnit::Em`: `raise = v * get_font_size(style)`.
- `LengthUnit::Rem`: `raise = v * get_font_size(style)` (rem is resolved to the element's font-size here;

- `LengthUnit::Vw | Vh`: out of scope here — leave a `// TODO(spec):` and treat as `0.0` (no shift).
